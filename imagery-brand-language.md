# RouteToRecall Imagery Brand Language

## Direction: Living Journeys

RouteToRecall photography should feel observed rather than staged: real Indian travellers absorbed in a place, with the destination carrying as much visual weight as the people.

## Visual Formula

- 60% candid human energy: walking, helping, laughing, noticing, moving, or sharing a small moment.
- 25% environmental scale: landscapes, architecture, weather, and local texture establish a strong sense of place.
- 15% controlled negative space: calm areas support Waveyu-style typography and responsive crops.
- Premium documentary-editorial photography with subtle 35mm grain.
- Natural movement in water, foliage, clothing, vehicles, or weather; faces stay readable.
- Deep moss and forest green, warm sand and ochre, charcoal, stone, cream, and softly muted blue.
- One restrained chartreuse-lime detail can connect the image to the interface palette.

## People and Casting

The image library must reflect varied, believable travel parties rather than repeating one aspirational couple:

- Friends of different genders and group sizes.
- Two women or two men travelling together.
- Couples across different adult age groups.
- Travellers in their 50s and 60s.
- Families with children.
- Multigenerational families.
- Solo travellers when the story benefits from solitude.

People should look Indian, age-authentic, individually dressed, and unaware of the camera. Avoid coordinated outfits, repeated faces, model-like posing, exaggerated expressions, and casting every destination with the same demographic.

## Composition

- Frame for both a 3:2 wide banner and a tall rounded destination-card crop.
- Keep important faces and gestures away from extreme edges.
- Use paths, waterlines, architecture, or landscape layers to create depth.
- Let people occupy a modest part of the frame unless intimacy is the story.
- Preserve useful negative space for large Bricolage Grotesque headlines.
- Keep cultural and geographic details accurate and respectful.

## Avoid

- Generic stock-tourism posing or influencer selfie aesthetics.
- Postcard copies of well-known photographs.
- Luxury clichés that overpower the destination experience.
- Excessive HDR, neon color, heavy teal-orange grading, or artificial lens flare.
- Fantasy architecture, unsafe travel behavior, visible brands, text, logos, and watermarks.
- Deliberately inserting every brand cue into every image; the system should feel consistent without feeling manufactured.

## Destination Set

- Thailand: three friends arriving by long-tail boat.
- Singapore: a young couple moving through a rain-fresh garden city.
- Malaysia: three friends exploring a George Town heritage street.
- Maldives: an older couple stepping from a local dhoni.
- Bali: a family with children walking through rice terraces.
- Sri Lanka: two women friends travelling by hill-country train.
- Nepal: three male friends helping one another on a Himalayan trail.
- Dubai: a multigenerational family approaching an abra at Dubai Creek.
- Cambodia: two older women friends discovering an Angkor-era temple.
